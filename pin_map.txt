TITRATOR PIN MAP  (source: config.h, target STM32G431CBU6)
=========================================================
 
BY FUNCTION
-----------
Pump driver (TMC2209, STEP/DIR mode)
  PA0   STEP_PIN           TMC2209 STEP
  PA1   DIR_PIN            TMC2209 DIR
  PA8   EN_PIN             TMC2209 EN  (enable level: LOW)
 
Limit switches (to GND, internal pull-up, pressed = LOW)
  PA3   LIMIT_EMPTY_PIN    plunger fully expelled (HOME / zero)
  PA4   LIMIT_FULL_PIN     plunger fully retracted (syringe full)
 
Servo (3-way valve)
  PA2   SERVO_PIN          SG90 or similar
                           0 deg = fill, 90 deg = dispense, 180 deg = rinse/waste
 
ADC ADS1256 (SPI1)
  PA5   SPI1 SCK
  PA6   SPI1 MISO          (ADS1256 DOUT)
  PA7   SPI1 MOSI          (ADS1256 DIN)
  PB0   ADS_CS_PIN         chip select
  PB1   ADS_DRDY_PIN       data ready, active LOW
  PB2   ADS_RESET_PIN      hardware reset (optional)
  --    ADS_PWDN_PIN       not used (-1), tie high in hardware
 
pH electrode inputs on the ADS1256
  AIN0  ADS_PH_CHANNEL     positive input
  AIN1  ADS_PH_NEG_CHANNEL negative input (differential mode is ON)
 
Serial
  USB CDC                  Serial over USB, 115200 baud (SERIAL_BAUD)
 
 
BY PIN
------
  PA0   TMC2209 STEP
  PA1   TMC2209 DIR
  PA2   Servo signal
  PA3   Limit switch EMPTY (HOME)
  PA4   Limit switch FULL
  PA5   SPI1 SCK  (ADS1256)
  PA6   SPI1 MISO (ADS1256 DOUT)
  PA7   SPI1 MOSI (ADS1256 DIN)
  PA8   TMC2209 EN
  PB0   ADS1256 CS
  PB1   ADS1256 DRDY
  PB2   ADS1256 RESET
 
 
NOT IN config.h, but taken by hardware (do not use for anything else)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  PA11 / PA12   USB D- / D+  (needed for USB CDC Serial)
  PA13 / PA14   SWD SWDIO / SWCLK  (ST-Link)
  NRST          not wired to the ST-Link on your setup (software reset)
